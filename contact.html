Feedback & corrections

Report a problem with a guide, sample file, or tool.

Shawn maintains this website. Corrections and general feedback are handled in the public GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account is required to post.

Report a correction on GitHub ↗

What to include

  • The page URL and the step or sentence involved.
  • A small fictional example that reproduces the problem.
  • The expected result and what actually happened.
  • For spreadsheet issues, the app version, platform and relevant regional settings.

Reports are public

Other people can read issues, comments and attachments. Do not post customer exports, credentials, account numbers, personal details or confidential business files. Replace real records with a minimal fictional example.

Other questions

Use the tracker for general questions about the site or its materials. For copyright concerns, give the affected page URL and identify the material without attaching private documents. Practice data may be adapted for learning and internal training; see the usage information.
