Make a delivery update specific without inventing certainty

Replace vague reassurance with a dated estimate, a clear purpose and verified authority.

Maintained by ShawnUpdated November 13, 20262 min read

The examples and answer keys on this page are authored teaching material. They are not recorded model outputs or claims about product performance.

Specificity comes from facts

“We value your patience and are working tirelessly to resolve this issue” offers little usable information. A useful delay update states which order is involved, what is known, what remains uncertain and the next available step.

Use the fictional delivery brief ↓

Here the carrier estimates 20 November 2026. The writer can communicate status, but cannot promise compensation. Customer name and order reference remain placeholders.

Compare the effect of three edits

WordingProblemBetter treatment
It will arrive on 20 NovemberTurns an estimate into a guaranteeCarrier currently estimates 20 November
We will refund youAuthority not suppliedOmit until authorized
Your order is important to usAdds little operational informationState the actual status and available next step

A calm tone can be brief. Removing inflated reassurance should not remove an appropriate apology or hide uncertainty.

Produce one reviewable message

Hello [name], the carrier currently estimates that order [reference] will arrive on 20 November 2026. The date may change. I’m sorry for the delay; please contact us if you need another status update.

This wording is authored for the exercise. Before sending a real version, verify the estimate, replace placeholders, check the recipient and confirm the sender can provide the stated follow-up.

Keep a small style rule

A team style rule might be: lead with the status, use ordinary words, avoid promises without authority, and include only a next step the team can perform. Test the rule on a cancellation or an absent carrier estimate as well as this easy case.

When the estimate is missing, say the date is not yet confirmed rather than filling the gap. Useful writing can be honest about an unresolved fact.
