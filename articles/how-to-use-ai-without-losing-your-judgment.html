Keep four decisions visible when AI helps with a report

Separate source meaning, transformation choices, calculation checks and final wording.

Maintained by ShawnUpdated November 13, 20262 min read

The examples and answer keys on this page are authored teaching material. They are not recorded model outputs or claims about product performance.

A report contains decisions before the final sentence

In the sales example, someone must decide what a row means, whether a repeated record is accidental, which dates and statuses count, and what claims the summary may make. An assistant can help explain those decisions, but silently accepting its assumptions changes the reporting question.

The source has a legitimate two-line order and a known accidental exact repeat. Treating both as the same kind of duplicate gives the wrong result.

Keep a short decision record

DecisionRule for the fictional sampleEvidence
Row meaningOne order lineDifferent line IDs within order 000101
Exact repeatRemove one known accidental recordData record 5 exactly repeats 4
Report scopeShipped; start included, end excludedExplicit exercise specification
NarrativeNo growth or causal claimNo comparison-period evidence

Keep the decision record with the workbook. A colleague should be able to reproduce the answer without seeing an AI conversation.

Investigate a conflicting suggestion

If an assistant suggests deleting all repeated order IDs, inspect the two lines in 000101. The pen line is worth RM9.60 and is genuine within the exercise. This counterexample provides a reason to reject that suggestion.

If a proposed formula disagrees with RM267.60, compare included records before adjusting the formula to force the target total. Matching a total by accident is not evidence that the row decisions are correct.

Hand over the method and its limits

Record the source file, rule choices, formula or tool version, expected checks and unresolved issues. Explain that the practice workbook covers a fixed prepared range and has no live source connection. A user extending it must update dependent ranges or use a reviewed table-based method.

The useful role of judgment is choosing and verifying these explicit rules. A polished final paragraph cannot replace them.
