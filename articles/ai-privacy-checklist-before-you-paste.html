Privacy

A privacy check for explaining changes between two exports

Decide whether to compare locally, share a summary, or use an approved data service.

Maintained by ShawnUpdated November 13, 20262 min read

The examples and answer keys on this page are authored teaching material. They are not recorded model outputs or claims about product performance.

The task is an explanation of differences

Suppose you need a short reconciliation note about two order snapshots. Start by comparing the files with a deterministic method. The assistant writing the explanation may need only reviewed findings, rather than the underlying customer records.

Use order-list comparison with order_id as the key. The expected result is two unchanged keys, one changed key, one only in each file, and one repeated-key group.

Choose the smallest input that answers the question

A sufficient writing brief could say: “One order changed from Pending to Shipped, one key appears only in each snapshot, and one repeated key needs review. Write a neutral reconciliation note. Do not infer refunds, cancellations or missing revenue.” No phone numbers or customer notes are needed.

Inspect both prompt and attachments

Check hidden sheets, notes, copied email headers and screenshots as well as visible names. Confirm that a file picker selected the intended teaching file. Do not assume a paid account establishes permission to upload workplace data.

This site cannot inspect your employer's agreements, a service's account settings or the sensitivity of your actual records. If the allowed processing is uncertain, stop the upload and ask the responsible owner.

Review what the generated note reveals

An output can disclose sensitive details too. Ensure the intended reader needs the summary and that its claims match the comparison. Keep unresolved repeated keys visible; the comparison does not authorize choosing or deleting a record.

The site's browser tools process inputs in memory, while hosting analytics receives website metrics. Read the current disclosure before using the live tools. An approved desktop application may be a better fit for restricted data.
