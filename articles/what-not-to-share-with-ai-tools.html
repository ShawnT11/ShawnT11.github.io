Reduce an order export to the fields needed for a formula question

Create a three-value fictional prompt instead of uploading customer identifiers, phones, and free-text notes.

Maintained by ShawnUpdated November 13, 20262 min read

Fictional practice case. The example prompts and answers are written for the exercise; they are not captured AI responses.

Ask what the assistant needs to know

The sales export has 11 columns, including customer codes, phone numbers and delivery notes. To explain net line value, the required facts are quantity, unit price and the meaning of the discount. Customer contact details contribute nothing to that calculation.

Inspect the fictional source columns ↓

Even when this site's sample is safe teaching data, the same upload habit may be inappropriate for a real export.

Build a smaller representation

Original fieldNeeded for formula explanation?Action
quantityYesUse a fictional value of 2
unit_price_rmYesUse 12.50
discount_rmYesUse 5.00 and state per line
phone / customer_codeNoOmit
noteNoOmit rather than paraphrase customer details

An authored prompt is: “A fictional line has quantity 2, unit price 12.50, and discount 5.00 once per line. Explain gross and net formulas.” The known answers are gross 25.00 and net 20.00.

Check what remains after removing names

Replacing a name with Customer A does not remove an unusual transaction history or a revealing free-text note. Review combinations of details, not just obvious identifiers. A screenshot can also expose other sheets, account information or file paths.

If the task needs only a schema, share invented column names and values. If it genuinely needs private records, use the organization's approved service and process. A generic checklist cannot determine whether your account or contract permits an upload.

Consider a local calculation first

The browser sales tool can calculate this sample without an AI upload endpoint. The live website does load hosting analytics; see the privacy disclosure. For sensitive work, use an approved local application and review its environment.

Write down the minimum input for the task before opening an assistant. That small decision is easier to repeat than cleaning an entire customer record after it has been shared.
