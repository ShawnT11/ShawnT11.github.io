Measure whether AI helps a weekly reporting task

Use a blank timing log, a common source and a completion gate instead of assuming a productivity gain.

Maintained by ShawnUpdated November 13, 20262 min read

Fictional practice case. The example prompts and answers are written for the exercise; they are not captured AI responses.

Agree on the finished result

Use the original fictional sales export for every method. A completed attempt must preserve identifiers, apply the documented duplicate rule, use the reporting window and produce RM267.60, 26 units, nine included lines and eight orders.

Blank measurement log ↓

The log intentionally contains no measured times. This article makes no claim that AI is faster.

Measure the whole attempt

StageInclude
SetupInstalling or configuring an approved method; learning the source
WorkImport, transformation, prompting and calculation
ReviewBoundary cases, identifier checks and reconciliation
CorrectionFixing failed or incomplete output
CompletionWhether every agreed output check passed

Do not compare an AI first draft with a colleague's fully reviewed report. A fast attempt that fails remains a failed attempt in the record.

Separate setup from repeatable work

Run a second trial using refresh-next-week.csv and the week starting 2 November. Its expected result is two lines, five units and RM85.00 net. This shows whether the first setup can be reused, rather than requiring the same cleanup again.

Record experience level and interruptions. Small trials are affected by familiarity and practice order. Rotate methods or repeat the task before treating a difference as a stable saving.

Use a conditional conclusion

A useful conclusion states the tested task, number of attempts, quality gate and actual observed time. If a method only helps draft explanations after calculations are complete, describe that narrower benefit.

Choose the method with acceptable quality, maintainability and data handling for the task. Keep a small failure case with the workflow so later changes can be checked.
