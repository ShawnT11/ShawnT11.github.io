The AI Explainer publishes practical spreadsheet workflows and careful uses of AI for everyday business work.

What you will find here

The site focuses on people who receive exports, reconcile lists, and prepare routine reports: office administrators, small-business operators, and anyone who needs a clear, repeatable spreadsheet process.

The workflow collection uses original fictional data, downloadable files, visible formulas, and expected answers. Browser tools support quick import checks, snapshot comparison, and date review. The AI case library connects AI concepts with routing, source checking, privacy, meeting notes and draft review. Each page has a distinct task or decision.

Who is responsible

The AI Explainer is an independent website owned and maintained by Shawn. Shawn is responsible for publication, maintenance, and corrections. Report a correction through the feedback page, with the page URL and a small fictional reproduction.

How this collection was made

AI assisted the creation of the workflow text, sample data, and code. The worked calculations and tool rules have reproducible checks. The examples do not represent a human expert's client work or a comparative benchmark of AI products. The verification page states the methods and their limits.

Why the examples are small

A small fictional dataset makes each decision visible. You can see why a repeated line is removed, why two lines still belong to one order, and why a boundary date changes a total. The goal is to make the check understandable before you apply a method to a larger file.
