Using the files

Every record and contact detail is fictional and created for these examples. You may use and adapt the practice data for learning and internal training. Attribution is appreciated. Third-party sources retain their own rights.

CSV has no spreadsheet column types. Import identifiers as Text and apply the declared date and decimal convention. The edge-case sample includes a deliberately formula-like note; inspect it through a text-typed import. The worked XLSX stores identifiers as text.

For a reusable workflow, read the guide's scope and test a second sample before replacing it with your own records. See how the examples are checked.
