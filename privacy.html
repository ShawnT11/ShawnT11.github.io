Privacy policy

How the site handles browser tools, hosting analytics, advertising, and public feedback.

Updated November 13, 2026.

Files used in browser tools

The four spreadsheet tools read pasted text or local files in browser memory. They do not upload file contents, store them in browser storage, or include them in URLs. The tool application has no file-upload endpoint and does not send CSV contents as part of its calculations. Tool pages do not include Google advertising scripts. Leaving or reloading the page clears the current tool state. Downloads are generated locally on your device.

Cloudflare hosting analytics

The live host injects Cloudflare Web Analytics into website pages, including the four spreadsheet tools. The beacon makes network requests to report visits and page-performance measurements. Cloudflare describes its collection in Data origin and collection and its privacy policy. These hosting requests are separate from the tool calculations. The prior statement that these pages loaded no analytics was corrected on November 13, 2026.

Other website requests

The hosting provider necessarily receives ordinary requests for pages and assets, including standard technical information such as IP address and browser information. Editorial article pages may load Google AdSense code. Some earlier helper pages also load Google-hosted fonts. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery and measurement.

Google explains its processing at how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Browser and Google account settings offer controls over cookies and ad personalization.

Public feedback on GitHub

Corrections and general questions use the public repository issue tracker. GitHub receives the account information and content you submit under its privacy statement. An issue, its comments and any attachments can be viewed by other people. Do not post private customer files, passwords, account details or personal information.

Privacy questions

Use the feedback page for general questions about this site. Keep any public question free of personal details. The linked Cloudflare and Google policies explain their own processing and available privacy controls.
