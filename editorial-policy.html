Editorial policy

How practical examples, sources, AI assistance, and corrections are handled.

Updated November 4, 2026.

Publish a complete, useful task

Workflow pages should state their input, assumptions, method, expected output, and limitations. Sample data must be clearly labeled fictional. Repeated general advice should not substitute for the page's promised example.

Make evidence traceable

Use official product documentation for feature-dependent claims and keep original calculations inspectable. A source link is background support; it does not replace testing a proposed formula or tool. State which checks were performed and avoid claiming tests that were not run.

Use AI transparently

AI may assist with writing, development, and sample creation. Do not invent personal experience, qualifications, user feedback, timings, screenshots, or product benchmark results. An illustrative faulty formula must be labeled as an instructional counterexample.

Keep changes and advertising clear

Correct substantive mistakes, update affected examples, and use honest publication dates. Advertising or commercial relationships must be distinguishable from tool controls and editorial recommendations. Tool pages in this collection do not load advertising scripts.

Corrections

Use the feedback page with a page URL and a specific fictional reproduction. See how the examples are checked for the current verification scope.
